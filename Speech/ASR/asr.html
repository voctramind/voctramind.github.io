From the foundations of recognition to CTC, transducers, attention, streaming, multilingual and multimodal systems — a sequential journey through modern ASR.

01

What does an ASR system actually have to model?

Before choosing a neural architecture, it helps to separate the ingredients of the recognition problem.

ACOUSTIC MODELINGRelating acoustic observations to linguistic units.
LANGUAGE MODELINGRepresenting which sequences of linguistic symbols are plausible.
PRONUNCIATION / LEXICONConnecting linguistic units with their pronunciations.
ALIGNMENTRelating a long sequence of acoustic frames to a shorter transcription.

The central difficulty is already visible: speech arrives as a dense temporal signal, while the transcript is a much shorter sequence of symbols.
